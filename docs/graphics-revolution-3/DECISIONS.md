# Technical decisions: Graphics Revolution 3.0

| # | Decision | Why | Alternative rejected |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Start from MakeHuman's CC0 base mesh, targets, skeleton and weights, not from primitives | The old body was smoothed ellipsoids and capsules: no real topology, no fingers, no face planes. More primitives would have been more polygons on the same mistake. Real anatomy is what made him stop looking like a mannequin | Bigger SDF body; buying a character (needs your authorization); AI-generated meshes (no rig, unclear rights) |
| 2 | Use only MakeHuman's data files, pinned to one commit with checksums; never its AGPL program code or MPFB | LICENSE.md sections C and D release assets as CC0 and make no claim on output. Keeps the game's license situation simple | Installing MPFB |
| 3 | 43 bones merged from MakeHuman's 163, keeping its authored weights | Adds what the 13-bone rig could not do: spine curve, neck, clavicles, wrists, forearm twist, 10 finger bones a hand, toes. No face or toe-segment bones nobody can see | Keep 13 bones; use all 163 |
| 4 | Keep coded clips, not authored `AnimationMixer` clips | Preserves the project's rule that animation derives from simulation state and works at any speed or after any load. Worker props and clip names are unchanged, so LiveEntities and the simulation did not change | Baked clips: they cannot put a hand on a rung whose position varies per roof |
| 5 | Late IK: limbs solved after rotations are blended | Blended IK lags, which is why hands used to float off rungs. Now contact is exact whenever in reach, and reports a measured shortfall when not | Blending IK results |
| 6 | Walking driven by distance covered, read from the character's real motion | A planted foot moves backward at exactly ground speed, so it cannot slide at 1×, 2× or 5× | Time-driven cycle with a guessed rate |
| 7 | Clothes are shells of the body, and the skin under them is deleted | Nothing can poke through, because nothing is there. Saves triangles too | Layered clothes over a full body |
| 8 | One atlas, one material, one merged mesh per character | 1 draw call each, against about 10 before plus decals and gloves. Colour per region through vertex colours | Per-region materials |
| 9 | The tint mask lives in the ORM map's green channel | Canvas encoding destroys colour under alpha; lossy WebP subsamples chroma, which turned a blue-channel mask to mush and broke the logo. Green is mostly luma | Alpha mask; blue channel (both tried, both failed visibly) |
| 10 | WebP textures, not KTX2 | KTX2 needs the `toktx` encoder, not installed; WebP is decoded by every target browser. Cost: textures are uncompressed in GPU memory. KTX2 would cut GPU memory about 4× for the 2048 set, and remains the next optimization if real phones show memory pressure | Installing native tooling without need; see PIPELINE.md for numbers |
| 11 | Meshopt lossless, no quantization | Same as the existing pipeline; the loader already supports it. Quantization would rewrite node transforms on a skinned asset for a small gain | `KHR_mesh_quantization` |
| 12 | Hair under a cap is painted on the scalp; fuller styles are shells | Shell edges were spiky at the hairline under a cap (a defect the brief names). Hair cards would need alpha sorting | Hair cards; capped shell |
| 13 | Field staff bare-handed, not gloved | The brief asks for human hands with fingers first, and gloves "where appropriate". Gloved hands could be a later variant | Always gloved, as before |
| 14 | Ladder off the rack: rear step, near-end grip, and on tall racks a drop-down tilt | On a 2.2 to 3.7 m rack the old rig's hands simply stopped short in the air. A technician uses the step; tall rigs need a drop-down rack | Stretching arms; leaving the hands short |
| 15 | Old `technician.glb` and `technician.py` stay in the repo, unused | Rollback path: `git revert` of this branch restores it with nothing to regenerate | Deleting them |
| 16 | Height 1.80 m, ankle 7.5 cm, hips 96 cm, from the mesh's own proportions, with choreography adjusted to suit | Old constants (ankle 3 cm, shoulder 1.51 m) were the old mannequin's. Shoulder rail, rung and step positions were updated | Forcing the mesh onto old constants |
| 17 | Posture for reaching is one pure function (`reachPosture.ts`), used by the character and by the reach test | The test measures what the character actually does, so a body change cannot silently break the choreography | Separate constants in the test |
| 18 | Walkers follow a height function from the street layout, shared with the road geometry; the simulation stays flat | Fixes sunken boots with no effect on saves, determinism or economics | Raising paths in the simulation; lowering sidewalks |
| 19 | Benchmarks run with vsync and the frame limit off, with quality pinned; a 60 Hz run only answers "are frames dropped?" | A capped run reads 16.7 ms whatever the cost, and an automatic step-down would compare different presets | Capped comparison |
| 20 | Kept the heavier ten-technician frames unfixed | Hiding the characters leaves them unchanged, so changing the character would not help; no frame is dropped at 60 Hz here | Reducing character quality on a guess |
